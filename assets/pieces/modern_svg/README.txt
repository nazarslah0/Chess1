Chess2 Classic SVG Pieces
12 individual SVG files: white_king.svg, white_queen.svg, white_rook.svg,
white_bishop.svg, white_knight.svg, white_pawn.svg and black equivalents.
Also includes chess_pieces_sprite.svg containing reusable symbols.

Flutter usage:
1. Add flutter_svg to pubspec.yaml dependencies.
2. Put the SVGs in assets/chess_pieces/ and declare the folder under flutter assets.
3. Render an individual piece with SvgPicture.asset('assets/chess_pieces/white_king.svg').

These are original vector drawings in a classic Staunton-inspired style, not copied Chess.com assets.
